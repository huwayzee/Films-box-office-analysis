What to do with this

  1. Treat releases as a portfolio: fund several low-budget films for each tentpole.
  2. Approve $100M+ projects only with proven IP, a family-friendly rating plan and international pre-sales.
  3. Aim for PG or PG-13 where the story allows. Keep R for low-budget genres such as horror.
  4. Spend on event-style marketing that builds word of mouth, rather than on production scale.
